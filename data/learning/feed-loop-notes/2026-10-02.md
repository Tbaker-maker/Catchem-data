## Mechanics I refused

- Wait for a filter before the feed has variety. Unusual Whales stays red until the user narrows the tape. This build is the unfiltered mix.
- One percent leaderboard for singles and sealed together. That is how the previous lead became 24 ninety-day singles, starting at Trubbish. Sealed is picked from sealed qualifiers and singles from single qualifiers, then interleaved. A box is not sorted against a single on percent.
- Dollar-move rank (price change times copies sold) as the gate. counts.json says there is no sold list. eBay completed sales did not land. A sealed product earns a seat from its own 7, 30, or 90 day market-ask window.
- Treating a listing count as a sold count.
- Inventing a 7-day or 90-day sold count. The only sold fact used is a 3-month TCGplayer product-page total already on that SKU in trending.json (commit 44cd6da). Surging Sparks keeps the 2026-10-01 print. The other rows on that file are the 2026-10-02 product-page print. The line says 3-month, TCGplayer, product page, not 7-day, not eBay. Products that are not in the file get no sold number.
- Averaging two price copies into a new "cleaned" price. A copy about 50% or more under the cluster is dropped. If the two copies are the same size, the product stays out of the lead.
- A shared frame: "a day in the week is missing" plus "this 90-day window opened" on every card. A missing day is mentioned only when that product has a multi-day weekday gap, with the dates.
- Pull rate, pack price, rip cost, and "more listed than sold." Those are not in the series.
- News lines with no source. Catalyst notes in the repo are not a dated source on a product id. Zero news slots.

## What I kept

Each line leads with the one fact that is different on that product's own points: a smaller or larger step, a higher high, a lower low, a flat stretch, a round trip, or a real gap. Then one watch, or one real extra (a listing count at or above 20, or the 3-month product-page sold total when the SKU is in trending.json). The percent is not repeated. The field stays `card.path`.

# Catch'em — IP Counsel Brief
*Prepared Aug 2026 for an initial consultation. Facts about our product are
accurate; nothing here is our legal conclusion — the numbered questions are
the consult.*

## 1. What the business is
Catch'em (catchemtcg.com) is a data + editorial platform for Pokémon TCG
collectors, operated by Tyler Baker (sole founder, US/Canada — BC-based).
Live or imminent: automated price tracking for sealed products and single
cards (public data sources), a market-analysis newsletter ("Catch'Em
News"), and social content. Currently free; future paid tier and a
collector marketplace are roadmapped (year 2). We are not affiliated with
Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, or TPCi, and never claim to be.

## 2. How Pokémon IP touches the product (exposure surfaces)
a. **Brand name + tagline.** "Catch'em" and the tagline "Catch'em. Catch
   Feels." — obvious proximity to the "Gotta Catch 'Em All" family of
   marks. Domain: catchemtcg.com ("tcg" in-string).
b. **Nominative references.** Card, set, and character names used to
   identify real products we report prices/analysis on ("Umbreon VMAX,"
   "Evolving Skies"), plus community slang ("Moonbreon").
c. **Imagery.** Card images and set logos sourced via the pokemontcg.io
   API for identification purposes in newsletter/site mockups. We do not
   use official marketing/key art (internal policy already bans it).
d. **Factual market data.** Prices, listing counts, market-state analysis
   about Pokémon products — our own measurements and editorial reads.
e. **Future/roadmapped (disclosure, not yet live):** (i) an app with
   original founder-drawn "meme card" characters that reference Pokémon
   (parody/homage register); (ii) a marketplace facilitating collector
   sales of genuine cards; (iii) paid subscriptions.

## 3. What we deliberately do not do
No reproduction of card scans as a product; no sale of Pokémon IP; no
official assets; no affiliation claims; no use of the full slogan "Gotta
Catch 'Em All" anywhere.

## 4. Questions for counsel (the consult)
1. **Name/tagline risk (top priority):** Is "Catch'em" + "Catch Feels"
   defensible against the catchphrase mark family? Risk-rank it and, if
   material, outline rebrand timing/paths before public launch scales.
2. Boundaries of **nominative fair use** for names + slang in a commercial
   data/editorial product (US + Canada exposure).
3. **Card image display:** what's safely displayable (thumbnails,
   dimensions, counts, linking-only alternatives), and whether relying on
   a third-party API's feed changes our position.
4. Same question for **set logos**.
5. **Original Pokémon-referencing characters** (future app): where the
   parody/derivative line sits for founder-drawn homage art sold inside a
   paid product.
6. **Marketplace** (future): platform posture for facilitating genuine-
   goods sales (first-sale doctrine, listing images, fee-taking).
7. **Disclaimers/notices:** exact trademark-attribution and
   non-affiliation language for site, newsletter, and socials.
8. **Housekeeping:** anything about the domain, "TCG" usage, or Canadian
   vs US exposure we should fix cheaply now.

## 5. Context (not offered as legal cover)
Comparable unaffiliated operations exist publicly (TCGplayer price data,
PriceCharting, PokeBeach editorial, Collectr et al.). Listed for market
context only.

## 6. What we want from the engagement
A ~60-minute consult; then (i) a short risk-ranked memo across §2, (ii)
go/no-go or remediation on the name/tagline, (iii) disclaimer language we
can paste. Founder is available evenings/weekends (two jobs); email-first.

🏆 Grader's corner

A reminder that costs people money every week: on established sets, a PSA 9 usually returns less than the raw card plus the grading fee. Deep 9 populations mean buyers only pay up for the 10, so the grade outcome behaves like a lottery where one number wins READ.

Fresh sets bend this — while graded copies are scarce, even 9s can clear raw-plus-fee — and that bend fades as populations fill in. If established-set 9s ever start persistently clearing the fee across our premium table, that's our falsifier and we'll say so in public. How we measure the premium →

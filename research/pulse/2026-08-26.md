## 💡 Did you know?
**"Shadowless" was never a planned edition.**

When the English Base Set launched in January 1999, the artwork window sat flat against the yellow border — no shadow. A short way into the print run, Wizards of the Coast added a thin grey shadow down the right side and along the bottom of that window. Every card printed before that change lacks it; every card after has it. Collectors named the earlier ones "Shadowless" long afterward.

So you can sort a pile of Base Set cards in seconds: look at the right edge of the picture box. A clean edge means it came from those first few weeks, and those copies sell for meaningfully more than the ones printed for the two years that followed.

*Source: Bulbapedia: 1st Edition (TCG) / Shadowless; Bulbapedia: Base Set (TCG), checked 2026-08-21.*

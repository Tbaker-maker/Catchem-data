## 💡 Did you know?
**There is a Base Set Charizard with a holo pattern that never went into production.**

CGC authenticated a 1999 Base Set Charizard test print that is missing pieces every normal card has — no Charmeleon in the evolution box, no flavour text under the art, no energy symbols. It also carries a shimmering foil pattern that appears on no regular Pokémon card. Test prints like it were usually never cut from the sheet, and when they were, they skipped the corner-rounding step, so the corners are square.

So square corners plus missing card elements is not damage or a fake — on rare occasions it is a factory test sheet that escaped, and graders treat it as its own category.

*Source: CGC Trading Cards: certification article on the Base Set Charizard test print, checked 2026-08-22.*

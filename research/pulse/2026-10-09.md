## 💡 Did you know?
**Charizard is card 4 of 102 because of the alphabet.**

The English Base Set was ordered by card type first, then by how rare the card was, then alphabetically by name. Among the holo rares that put Alakazam at 1, Blastoise at 2, Chansey at 3 — and Charizard at 4.

So the most recognisable number in the hobby, 4/102, is not a designation of importance. It is where the letter C landed.

*Source: Bulbapedia: Base Set (TCG) — English release ordering; Bill's Archive: Base Set complete guide, checked 2026-08-22.*

## 💡 Did you know?
**There is a brand-new Base Set Charizard you cannot play with.**

The original 1999 Charizard artwork was reprinted for the Charizard & Ho-Oh deck in Pokémon Trading Card Game Classic. That reprint carries no regulation mark — the small letter modern cards use to show which seasons they are allowed in — so it cannot be used in sanctioned tournament play.

So if you are checking whether any modern card is tournament-eligible, look for a single small letter near the bottom of the card. No letter means it was never meant for competition, whatever the artwork says.

*Source: Bulbapedia: Charizard (Base Set 4) — reprints section, checked 2026-08-22.*

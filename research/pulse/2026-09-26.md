## 💡 Did you know?
**Nobody actually knows how many 1st Edition Base Set cards were printed.**

The figure repeated everywhere is 10,000 copies of each card. It has never been confirmed by Wizards of the Coast or The Pokémon Company. It circulates as community lore, not documented fact.

So the next time someone quotes a print-run number at you as though it settles an argument, remember that the most repeated figure in the hobby has no source behind it.

## 💡 Did you know?
**The very first Pokémon cards had no rarity symbol at all — and a printing mistake.**

Japan's original Expansion Pack from 1996 exists in two forms: with a rarity symbol in the corner, and without one. The symbol-less version is believed to be the earlier print run, because it carries errors that were later corrected — Venusaur was printed as Pokémon number 68 instead of 3. Collectors call it the No Rarity Base Set.

So if you ever handle a Japanese Base Set card, check the bottom-right corner of the art. Nothing there means you're holding one of the earliest Pokémon cards ever printed, mistakes and all.

*Source: Bulbapedia: Base Set (TCG) — Japanese release section, checked 2026-08-22.*

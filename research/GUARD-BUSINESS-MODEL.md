# Catch'em Guards — the model, audited

**Researched 2026-08-23. Every market fact below is sourced; every unknown is
marked UNKNOWN rather than estimated.** The gap between observable retail prices
and unobservable costs is where invented business models live, and a fiction here
is more dangerous than one in a post because somebody spends money on it.

---

## THE MARKET IS REAL, CROWDED, AND HAS ONE HOLE

| player | material | position |
|---|---|---|
| **GradedGuard** | TPU + polycarbonate | colourways, hobby-store exclusives, storage for 12 and 36 |
| **Slabmags** | aluminium alloy + tempered glass | premium, every grader, 99.9% UV claimed |
| **Phantom Display** | cast acrylic | display-led, Magneto **$55–60** (July 2026) |
| **Amazon field** | TPU | VALUN, X PRO, SlabX, RCPLAGA, Livresse — 4 to 12 packs |
| **Etsy field** | 3D printed | custom colours, low volume, profitable at small scale |

**THE HOLE: every one of them sells colourways you CHOOSE.** Not one sells
rarity, a pack, or a pull. **And GradedGuard already runs hobby-store
exclusives** — which means scarcity is proven to work in this exact category
before we test it ourselves.

## THE TWO THINGS THAT MAKE OURS DIFFERENT

**1 · The pack, not the picker.** Five guards, printed odds, the slab guard as
the hit. Nobody in this category has mapped the hobby's own core loop onto the
accessory that sits beside it.

**2 · The slab guard is a DIFFERENT PRODUCT, not a rarer colour.** Fitting a
graded slab rather than a raw card is why it reads as a pull instead of a
gradient. A rare colour is a nicer version of what you already got; a slab guard
is the thing you did not have.

## THE STRUCTURE, FIXED
Per Tyler's ruling: **published odds, guaranteed tangible good, no buy-back.**
Every pack yields five real guards. That is the blind-box structure already on
every toy-shop shelf, and removing the cash-out removed the only element that
made it read as anything else.

## RARITY — a proposal, not a decision
| tier | odds | why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| Core | 60% | the everyday guard. **If this one does not feel good, nothing else matters** |
| Colourway | 26% | the reason somebody buys a second pack |
| Metal | 11% | different material, visibly heavier in the hand |
| **Slab Guard** | **3%** | **the hit — a different product, roughly 1 in 33** |

Odds are a design decision and should be set against a real cost sheet, not
before one.

## WHAT WE DO NOT KNOW — recorded, not estimated

`data/knowledge.json` → `slab-guard-costs-unknown`, confidence **UNKNOWN**:

**Manufacturing cost, tooling cost, minimum order quantity and landed unit
economics are all unknown for every material tier.** Retail prices are
observable; costs are not. **Any margin figure produced before a quote exists is
fiction.**

### THE THREE QUESTIONS THAT DECIDE EVERYTHING
1. **What does a TPU guard cost at 500 units, and at 5,000?** Injection moulding
   has tooling; the tool is the barrier, not the plastic.
2. **What is the MOQ?** This decides whether a first run is $800 or $18,000, and
   therefore whether it is a weekend or a loan.
3. **Does 3D printing carry a first run?** Etsy sellers do it profitably at low
   volume, which suggests **a pilot needs no tooling and no minimum** — the two
   things that normally make a physical product impossible for a solo founder.

## THE ROUTE THAT DOES NOT NEED A LOAN
**Print the first run.** No tooling, no MOQ, no inventory risk. Sell fifty packs
to the community that already exists. **The pilot's job is not profit — it is a
real number for every unknown above**, and fifty packs buys that number for
roughly the cost of a night out.

Injection moulding becomes a decision **after** demand is evidenced, not before.

## HOW IT CONNECTS TO WHAT ALREADY EXISTS
- **The editor renders the slab.** Somebody can see their card in a guard before
  buying one — the only tool in this category that can.
- **The rip is built** (`research/assets/rip.html`) with printed odds and the
  hit revealed last.
- **The binder page is the display case.** Plan nine slabs and their colours
  before spending anything.
- **Streaks give a daily reason to be here**, which is where guards get seen.
- **Berries are earned and unpurchasable**, so a guard obtainable through
  participation keeps the community lane open.

# Catch'em Card Art Prompts — AI Image Generation

> **Purpose:** Ready-to-paste AI art prompts for every Catch'em character archetype, plus 1-3 attacks/abilities per card. Built for Midjourney and DALL-E (natural language style).

> **Status:** v0.1 — 2026-04-22. All 84 archetype seeds from the brainstorm doc.

**IMPORTANT — HOW TO USE THIS FILE:**
1. Pick a character.
2. Copy the full prompt block (marked `PROMPT`).
3. Paste into your AI image tool.
4. Regenerate 2-5 times — variance is normal. Pick the version closest to on-vibe.
5. Export at 750×1000 px, transparent background, `.png`.
6. Save using filename convention: `s{season#}-{character-slug}.png`

---

## Style Foundation (appears at end of every prompt)

This block appears at the end of every single prompt in this file. It's what makes all 84 characters feel like one unified Catch'em collection.

**THE STYLE BLOCK:**

**Use that exact text at the end of every prompt** — consistency across the set depends on it.

## Source Material Reference (for your mental model, not the prompts)

Catch'em characters draw from real-world creatures and mythology — the same source library Pokemon used. Fire dragons have existed in Chinese, Norse, Welsh, and fantasy traditions for centuries. Sea turtles are real animals. Ghost spirits appear across every culture's folklore. This is shared public-domain archetype territory.

Prompts in this file describe characters by their **source archetype** (fire dragon, small water turtle, ghost spirit, electric rodent), never by referencing a specific Pokemon.

# HOW TO REGENERATE / ITERATE

**If a generated image is off-vibe:**
- **Too cute, no anxiety?** → Add "slightly worried expression, one visible sweat drop"
- **Too dark/edgy?** → Add "wholesome chibi cartoon, soft palette"
- **Style not matching other cards?** → Re-emphasize "flat colors, bold marker outlines, no gradients, Moomin aesthetic"
- **Character too specific-looking?** → Remove specificity; use more generic archetype terms

**If it's producing something Pokemon-adjacent anyway:**
- Remove any creature-specific terms ("dragon" → "flame-spirit," "turtle" → "shelled-creature")
- Add "original character, storybook creature"
- Change coloring to non-obvious palette

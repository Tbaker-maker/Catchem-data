# Catch'em Seasons & Characters — Naming Brainstorm

> **Purpose:** A living document for naming Catch'em's seasons and generating character archetype seeds. Not a spec. Not canon. A thinking tool.

> **Status:** v0.1 — started 2026-04-22. Tyler's call on everything here.

**Voice:** Soft absurd with anxiety. Little guys trying their best. Earnest weirdness. Cute with a wink.

---

## 0. Voice Foundation (read first, re-read often)

### What Catch'em characters ARE

- **Soft little guys.** Characters who care intensely about small things. Who are a little anxious. Who are trying their best.
- **Earnest.** They're not being ironic. They're *being*. The humor comes from the sincerity.
- **Mixed cast.** Some are creatures. Some are objects with feelings. Some are trainers/humans with quiet worries. The "Catch'em universe" is populated by all of them together.
- **Quietly absurd.** A snail who keeps a ledger. A moth who bought at the peak. A tiny ghost who won't sell their mint 1999 Gameboy. The absurdity is presented without winking.
- **Lovable even when tragic.** A card about buying the top is a character to care about, not a target to mock.
- **Rare cards can be pointed.** The `legendary` tier can be a little more specific, a little more knowing. Commons stay gentle.

### What Catch'em characters are NOT

- ❌ **Cynical.** We're not "I Bought the Top"-roasting-the-dude. The voice skews tender.
- ❌ **Pepe / Wojak / Gigachad.** The meme reference pool is not 4chan-adjacent.
- ❌ **Corporate-cute.** Not Duolingo-owl-threatening. Not forced charm.
- ❌ **Edgy ironic.** No crypto-twitter energy. No detachment.
- ❌ **Punchline-shaped.** Names aren't punchlines. They're tiny portraits.
- ❌ **Flatly descriptive.** "Pack Ripper" is too on-the-nose. "The Ripper Who Meant It" has more life.

### The "soft absurd with anxiety" test

A name passes if it could be:
- A Moomin side character
- A Toot & Puddle friend who shows up in book 3
- A name in a Richard Scarry busytown scene
- A character in a Studio Ghibli short nobody's seen
- Something a little kid would invent while playing alone

A name fails if it sounds like:
- A Robinhood meme-stock tweet
- A Reddit flair
- A shitcoin project
- A corporate mascot
- The name of a Twitter-bro's podcast

### Flavor text rules (some cards, not all)

- Short. 8-12 words maximum.
- Reads like **a tiny thought the character is having.**
- Present tense.
- No punchlines.
- Example:
  - `Name: The Snail Who Wrote It Down`
  - `Flavor: "My spreadsheet is in a safer place than my cards."`
- Most commons don't need flavor. Epics and legendaries usually do.

### Rarity → tone mapping

| Rarity | Tone |
|---|---|
| Common | Gentle. Observational. "A little guy exists." |
| Rare | Earnest worry. "A little guy is concerned about a specific thing." |
| Epic | Specific character moment. "A little guy is *in* a situation." |
| Legendary | Can be pointed. Can reference market moments. Still loving. |

## 2. Character Archetype Seeds

Organized by season. 6-8 seeds per season. **All tagged `[SEED]`** — nothing here is locked. The goal is starter clay, not finished sculptures.

Each archetype includes:
- **Name** — working title
- **What they are** — one line
- **Why this era** — cultural fit
- **Vibe tier** — soft / earnest / melancholic
- **Potential flavor text** — if any

Most seeds are characters or creatures. A few are objects or trainers as you requested. Skew is toward the soft absurd with anxiety voice.

## 6. Voice Drift Watchlist

**Phrases and vibes to avoid** so Catch'em voice doesn't drift.

### Words that break the voice
- "Bro" / "bros" (crypto-twitter)
- "Chad" / "Gigachad" (Wojak-adjacent)
- "Based" / "cringe" (irony-poisoned)
- "Vibes" used as a noun alone ("give me the vibes") — okay in writing about vibe, not as name
- "Pepe" / anything froglike with Pepe energy
- "Literally" used in memey way
- Corporate adjectives ("delightful," "magical," "awesome," "incredible")
- Generic-startup adjectives ("powerful," "intuitive," "seamless")

### Patterns that break the voice
- Names that are a punchline (e.g., "The Guy Who Is Bad At Stonks")
- Names that roast the user in a cynical way
- Names that are acronyms (feels corporate)
- Names with "the" + literal descriptor (e.g., "The Collector") unless modified
- Pokemon character names, species names, or move names — even as memes
- References to real companies (Beckett, PSA, TCGPlayer) as character identity

### Signs the voice is drifting toward cynicism
- If a name feels like a Reddit username, it's drifted
- If a name feels like a Twitter post, it's drifted
- If you'd say "bro this card is SO accurate" about it, it's drifted
- Return to: "would this character exist in a gentle picture book?"

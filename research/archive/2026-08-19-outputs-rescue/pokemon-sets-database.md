# Catch'em Pokémon TCG English Sets Database

*A human-readable companion to `pokemon-sets-database.json`. Last updated: 2026-04-21*

---

## Scope and sources

English-language main expansions and notable special sets only. Japanese-exclusive sets, McDonald's promos, and POP series promo sets excluded.

**Primary sources cross-verified:** Bulbapedia, Wikipedia (List of Pokémon Trading Card Game sets), Beckett Pokemon TCG Expansion List, Wargamer complete set list, Bill's Archive historical schedule analysis, TCG Collector international sets database

**Flagged entries** (`flag_review: true` in JSON) have at least one piece of data that wasn't fully cross-verified at build time — usually because the set is recent/upcoming or has pre-release info only. Verify before using these in public-facing content.

## Era 1: WOTC Era (Original Series + E-Card)

**Period:** 1999-2003  
**Publisher:** Wizards of the Coast  
**Set count:** 15

*Foundation era. Short print runs, iconic art, vintage-grade scarcity. Final sets (Skyridge especially) are among the rarest in the hobby.*

### Base Set (BS)

**Released:** 1999-01-09 · **102 cards** · *Original Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `iconic` · `grail`

**Mechanics:** original game format

**Notable cards:** Charizard (4/102), Blastoise (2/102), Venusaur (15/102), Chansey (3/102)

The first set. 1st Edition and Shadowless prints carry massive premiums. Charizard is the defining vintage grail.

### Jungle (JU)

**Released:** 1999-06-16 · **64 cards** · *Original Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `iconic`

**Notable cards:** Snorlax, Scyther, Mr. Mime, Flareon, Jolteon, Vaporeon

Second English set. Introduced the original Eeveelutions as holos. 1st Edition prints premium.

### Fossil (FO)

**Released:** 1999-10-10 · **62 cards** · *Original Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `iconic`

**Notable cards:** Dragonite, Gengar, Lapras, Aerodactyl, Zapdos, Articuno

Third English set. First appearance of Gen 1 fossils in TCG. 1st Edition prints premium.

### Base Set 2 (B2)

**Released:** 2000-02-24 · **130 cards** · *Original Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `reprint`

**Notable cards:** Charizard, Blastoise, Venusaur (all reprints)

Reprint set combining highlights from Base Set and Jungle. Generally less desirable than originals, but clean holos still command value.

### Team Rocket (TR)

**Released:** 2000-04-24 · **83 cards** · *Original Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `iconic` · `theme`

**Mechanics:** Dark Pokemon debuted

**Notable cards:** Dark Charizard, Dark Blastoise, Dark Raichu (secret rare), Rocket's Zapdos, Here Comes Team Rocket!

Introduced 'Dark' Pokemon type. First secret rare in English TCG (Dark Raichu, 83/82). Strong collector base.

### Gym Heroes (G1)

**Released:** 2000-08-14 · **132 cards** · *Original Series (Gym)*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `theme`

**Mechanics:** Gym Leader Pokemon

**Notable cards:** Misty's Seadra, Erika's Dragonair, Sabrina's Gengar, Brock's Ninetales, Lt. Surge's Fearow, Misty's Tentacruel

First of the two-set Gym block. Themed around Kanto Gym Leaders. Holos widely available but specific cards (Misty's Tentacruel) valuable.

### Gym Challenge (G2)

**Released:** 2000-10-16 · **132 cards** · *Original Series (Gym)*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `theme` · `iconic`

**Notable cards:** Blaine's Charizard, Giovanni's Gyarados, Rocket's Mewtwo, Rocket's Moltres, Rocket's Zapdos, Sabrina's Gengar

Second Gym set. Contains Blaine's Charizard — one of the strongest vintage chase cards outside Base Set.

### Neo Genesis (N1)

**Released:** 2000-12-16 · **111 cards** · *Neo Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `gen2` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** Gen 2 Pokemon introduced, Darkness & Metal energy types, Baby Pokemon

**Notable cards:** Lugia, Typhlosion, Feraligatr, Meganium, Steelix

First Gen 2 / Johto set. Neo Genesis Lugia is one of the most sought-after modern vintage chases. 1st Edition prints premium.

### Neo Discovery (N2)

**Released:** 2001-06-01 · **75 cards** · *Neo Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `gen2` · `sleeper`

**Notable cards:** Espeon, Umbreon (first TCG appearance), Heracross, Politoed

First English TCG appearance of Espeon and Umbreon. Historical significance for Eeveelution collectors.

### Neo Revelation (N3)

**Released:** 2001-09-24 · **66 cards** · *Neo Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `scarce` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** First Shining Pokemon

**Notable cards:** Shining Magikarp, Shining Gyarados, Ho-Oh, Crobat

First Shining Pokemon cards in English TCG. Shining Gyarados and Shining Magikarp are defining chase cards. Low print run.

### Neo Destiny (N4)

**Released:** 2002-02-28 · **113 cards** · *Neo Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `scarce` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** Light Pokemon, more Shining Pokemon

**Notable cards:** Shining Charizard, Shining Mewtwo, Shining Raichu, Shining Tyranitar, Light Dragonite

Contains Shining Charizard — one of the most iconic and valuable vintage chase cards. Extremely scarce in gem mint condition.

### Legendary Collection (LC)

**Released:** 2002-05-24 · **110 cards** · *Legendary Collection*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `reprint` · `special`

**Mechanics:** Reverse holo rarity introduced

**Notable cards:** Charizard (reverse holo), Blastoise (reverse holo), Venusaur (reverse holo)

Reprint compilation of Base/Jungle/Fossil/Team Rocket highlights. Introduced reverse holo. Reverse holo Charizard is a hidden grail.

### Expedition Base Set (EX)

**Released:** 2002-09-15 · **165 cards** · *E-Card Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `experimental` · `scarce`

**Mechanics:** e-Reader dot codes, Supporter card type, Stadium card type

**Notable cards:** Charizard, Blastoise, Venusaur, Crystal Charizard (rare)

First e-Card set. Introduced Supporters and Stadiums as card types. New layout, slimmer borders. e-Reader compatibility required Japanese hardware in US.

### Aquapolis (AQ)

**Released:** 2003-01-15 · **186 cards** · *E-Card Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `experimental` · `scarce` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** Crystal Type Pokemon (first), e-Reader games

**Notable cards:** Crystal Charizard (holo), Crystal Lugia (holo), Crystal Nidoking, Crystal Kingdra, Crystal Ho-Oh

Introduced Crystal Type Pokemon — a collector-favorite rarity. Crystal Charizard from Aquapolis is a high-grade vintage chase. Scarce print.

### Skyridge (SK)

**Released:** 2003-05-12 · **144 cards** · *E-Card Series*

**Tags:** `vintage` · `grail` · `ultra-scarce` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** Crystal Type Pokemon (final), last WOTC set

**Notable cards:** Crystal Charizard, Crystal Celebi, Crystal Ho-Oh, Crystal Crobat, Crystal Kabutops, Crystal Golem

Last WOTC-published set. Extraordinarily low print run made Skyridge one of the rarest and most valuable modern vintage sets. Crystal Charizard is the grail. Sealed boxes sell for high 5 figures.

## Era 2: EX Era

**Period:** 2003-2007  
**Publisher:** The Pokémon Company International (TPCi)  
**Set count:** 16

*TPCi takes over from WOTC. Powerful Pokemon-ex mechanic introduces prize-card risk/reward. Delta Species and Gold Star chase cards are defining rarities of the era. Gen 3 (Hoenn) Pokemon central, with Gen 1-2 reprints through Delta Species treatment.*

### EX Ruby & Sapphire (RS)

**Released:** 2003-06-18 · **109 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `foundational`

**Mechanics:** Pokemon-ex debut, Gen 3 Pokemon

**Notable cards:** Blaziken ex, Sceptile ex, Swampert ex, Gardevoir ex

First set by TPCi (Pokemon USA). Introduced the Pokemon-ex mechanic. Defines the era's start.

### EX Sandstorm (SS)

**Released:** 2003-09-18 · **100 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern`

**Notable cards:** Crobat ex, Tyranitar ex, Dark Tyranitar, Crawdaunt ex

Second EX set. Themed around desert Pokemon.

### EX Dragon (DR)

**Released:** 2003-11-24 · **100 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `iconic`

**Notable cards:** Rayquaza ex, Latios ex, Latias ex, Salamence ex, Altaria ex

Dragon-themed EX set. First appearance of Rayquaza ex, a definitive chase card.

### EX Team Magma vs Team Aqua (MA)

**Released:** 2004-03-15 · **97 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `theme`

**Mechanics:** Team-branded Pokemon

**Notable cards:** Team Aqua's Kyogre ex, Team Magma's Groudon ex, Team Aqua's Manectric ex, Team Magma's Rhydon ex

Themed around the rival teams from Ruby/Sapphire games. Cards explicitly belong to one faction or the other.

### EX Hidden Legends (HL)

**Released:** 2004-06-14 · **102 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern`

**Notable cards:** Groudon ex, Kyogre ex, Regirock ex, Regice ex, Registeel ex

Features the Hoenn legendaries. Regi trio first TCG appearance.

### EX FireRed & LeafGreen (RG)

**Released:** 2004-08-30 · **116 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `nostalgia` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** Gen 1 returns

**Notable cards:** Charizard ex, Blastoise ex, Venusaur ex, Articuno ex, Zapdos ex, Moltres ex

First major Gen 1 callback set under TPCi. Kanto starters as ex. Strong collector nostalgia value.

### EX Team Rocket Returns (TRR)

**Released:** 2004-11-08 · **111 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `iconic` · `grail`

**Mechanics:** Gold Star Pokemon (first)

**Notable cards:** Rocket's Charizard ex, Rocket's Mewtwo ex, Gold Star Entei, Gold Star Raikou, Gold Star Suicune

Introduced the Gold Star rarity — shiny-variant Pokemon with starred rarity symbol. Gold Stars are among the most valuable EX-era chase cards. Legendary beasts trio as Gold Stars.

### EX Deoxys (DX)

**Released:** 2005-02-14 · **108 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern`

**Notable cards:** Rayquaza Gold Star, Deoxys ex (Normal/Attack/Defense/Speed forms)

Rayquaza Gold Star from this set is a defining EX-era grail. Deoxys featured in all four forms.

### EX Emerald (EM)

**Released:** 2005-05-09 · **107 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern`

**Notable cards:** Gyarados Gold Star, Magikarp Gold Star, Rayquaza ex, Flygon ex

Features Gold Star Magikarp and Gyarados — a paired collector's duo prized together.

### EX Unseen Forces (UF)

**Released:** 2005-08-22 · **145 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `iconic` · `grail`

**Mechanics:** Unown alphabet collection

**Notable cards:** Ho-Oh Gold Star, Espeon Gold Star, Umbreon Gold Star, Tyranitar ex, Unown collection (A-Z + ! + ?)

Contains Espeon and Umbreon Gold Stars — among the most valuable and coveted cards of the EX era. Full Unown alphabet + punctuation. Large set.

### EX Delta Species (DS)

**Released:** 2005-10-31 · **114 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `experimental`

**Mechanics:** Delta Species Pokemon (first), Holon Pokemon

**Notable cards:** Mewtwo Delta ex, Rayquaza Delta ex, Holon's Magnemite, Latios ex, Latias ex

Introduced Delta Species mechanic — Pokemon with alternate typings (e.g., Fire-type Pikachu). Delta Species defines the next four EX sets.

### EX Legend Maker (LM)

**Released:** 2006-02-13 · **93 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `sleeper`

**Mechanics:** continued Delta Species

**Notable cards:** Mew ex, Aerodactyl ex, Gardevoir ex Delta

Smaller set. Continues Delta Species line. Mew ex is the notable chase.

### EX Holon Phantoms (HP)

**Released:** 2006-05-03 · **111 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `grail`

**Mechanics:** Delta Species continued

**Notable cards:** Mewtwo Gold Star, Gyarados Gold Star (shiny variant), Pikachu Gold Star, Mew ex, Crawdaunt ex, Mightyena ex

Contains three Gold Stars including Pikachu Gold Star — one of the most sought-after Pikachu cards in the TCG.

### EX Crystal Guardians (CG)

**Released:** 2006-08-30 · **100 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `nostalgia`

**Mechanics:** Delta Species continued, Crystal-type references

**Notable cards:** Charizard ex Delta, Blastoise ex Delta, Venusaur ex Delta, Celebi ex, Jirachi ex

Second Kanto starter homage through Delta Species variants. Collector-favorite Delta cards.

### EX Dragon Frontiers (DF)

**Released:** 2006-11-08 · **101 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `grail`

**Mechanics:** Delta Species + Dragon theme

**Notable cards:** Rayquaza Gold Star Delta, Mew Gold Star Delta, Charizard Delta, Salamence ex

Contains Rayquaza Gold Star (Delta Species variant) — a distinct and valuable chase separate from EX Deoxys Rayquaza Gold Star.

### EX Power Keepers (PK)

**Released:** 2007-02-14 · **108 cards** · *EX Series*

**Tags:** `vintage-modern` · `closing-chapter`

**Mechanics:** last EX set

**Notable cards:** Latios ex, Latias ex, Gardevoir ex, Charizard, Mew

Final EX Series set and final Gen 3 set. Reprint-heavy. Loosely themed around Hoenn Elite Four.

## Era 3: Diamond & Pearl / Platinum Era

**Period:** 2007-2009  
**Publisher:** TPCi  
**Set count:** 11

*Gen 4 / Sinnoh era. Pokemon LV.X mechanic replaces ex. Lost Zone introduced in Platinum. Established the 4-sets-per-year quarterly cadence that still defines the modern schedule. Pokemon-SP subtype is the era's competitive hallmark.*

### Diamond & Pearl (DP)

**Released:** 2007-05-23 · **130 cards** · *Diamond & Pearl Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `foundational`

**Mechanics:** Pokemon LV.X debut, Gen 4 Pokemon, Darkness/Metal as basic energy

**Notable cards:** Dialga LV.X, Palkia LV.X, Empoleon LV.X, Torterra LV.X, Infernape LV.X

First Gen 4 / Sinnoh set. Introduced Pokemon LV.X mechanic. Release date varies by source (May 3 or May 23, 2007).

### Mysterious Treasures (MT)

**Released:** 2007-08-22 · **124 cards** · *Diamond & Pearl Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage`

**Mechanics:** Pokemon with Item cards

**Notable cards:** Honchkrow LV.X, Magmortar LV.X, Electivire LV.X, Luxray, Rhyperior

Second D&P set. Introduces 'Pokemon with Item' mechanic — Pokemon with held items integrated.

### Secret Wonders (SW)

**Released:** 2007-11-07 · **132 cards** · *Diamond & Pearl Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage`

**Notable cards:** Mewtwo LV.X, Darkrai, Blissey, Gardevoir

Third D&P set. Mewtwo LV.X is the featured chase. Strong competitive cards.

### Great Encounters (GE)

**Released:** 2008-02-13 · **106 cards** · *Diamond & Pearl Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `nostalgia`

**Notable cards:** Leafeon LV.X, Glaceon LV.X, Magmortar, Empoleon

Introduces Leafeon and Glaceon — the two newest Eeveelutions — to the TCG as LV.X chases.

### Majestic Dawn (MD)

**Released:** 2008-05-21 · **100 cards** · *Diamond & Pearl Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage`

**Mechanics:** Pokemon G subtype

**Notable cards:** Dialga G LV.X (first Pokemon G), Empoleon LV.X, Glaceon LV.X, Leafeon LV.X

Introduces Pokemon G subtype. Smaller set.

### Legends Awakened (LA)

**Released:** 2008-08-20 · **146 cards** · *Diamond & Pearl Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `large-set`

**Mechanics:** Technical Machines return

**Notable cards:** Shaymin LV.X, Regigigas LV.X, Heatran LV.X, Kabutops, Rampardos

Fourth-largest set in TCG history at the time. Seven Pokemon LV.X cards — more than any prior set.

### Stormfront (SF)

**Released:** 2008-11-05 · **100 cards** · *Diamond & Pearl Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** Shiny Pokemon return (alternate coloration)

**Notable cards:** Charizard, Dusknoir LV.X, Sableye (SF), Machamp

Final D&P-branded set. Stormfront Charizard is a fan-favorite chase. Sableye SF became a competitive staple (Turn 1 'Trick or Trap').

### Platinum (PL)

**Released:** 2009-02-11 · **127 cards** · *Platinum Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `mechanical-shift`

**Mechanics:** Lost Zone introduced, Pokemon SP subtype

**Notable cards:** Giratina LV.X, Porygon-Z LV.X, Regigigas LV.X, Dialga G, Palkia G

Introduced Lost Zone (cards removed from play permanently) and Pokemon-SP mechanic. Major competitive shift.

### Rising Rivals (RR)

**Released:** 2009-05-16 · **111 cards** · *Platinum Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage`

**Mechanics:** Pokemon GL / 4 / E4 subtypes

**Notable cards:** Flygon LV.X, Luxray GL LV.X, Gallade 4 LV.X, Shaymin

Expands Pokemon-SP subtypes. Luxray GL LV.X is competitive hallmark of the era.

### Supreme Victors (SV)

**Released:** 2009-08-19 · **147 cards** · *Platinum Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `large-set` · `competitive-era`

**Notable cards:** Garchomp C LV.X, Dialga G LV.X, Palmer's Contribution, Absol G LV.X

Garchomp C LV.X defines the late-SP meta. Largest D&P/Platinum set.

### Arceus (AR)

**Released:** 2009-11-04 · **99 cards** · *Platinum Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `closing-chapter`

**Mechanics:** Arceus forms

**Notable cards:** Arceus LV.X (three versions), Spiritomb, Shiny versions

Final Platinum set. Three Arceus LV.X variants (Fighting, Grass, Lightning). Spiritomb was the SP-killer of the meta.

## Era 4: HeartGold & SoulSilver Era

**Period:** 2010-2011  
**Publisher:** TPCi  
**Set count:** 5

*Short era (5 sets). Returns to Johto / Gen 2 roots with remakes as the theme. Pokemon Prime and LEGEND cards are the defining rarities — Prime has holo backgrounds, LEGEND cards are two-card sets that form one full artwork. Call of Legends closes the era with Shiny Pokemon reprints.*

### HeartGold & SoulSilver (HS)

**Released:** 2010-02-10 · **123 cards** · *HeartGold & SoulSilver Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `gen2-revival` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** Pokemon Prime (first), Pokemon LEGEND (first, two-card)

**Notable cards:** Lugia LEGEND, Ho-Oh LEGEND, Entei & Raikou LEGEND, Suicune & Entei LEGEND, Feraligatr Prime, Typhlosion Prime, Meganium Prime

Introduced Pokemon Prime and Pokemon LEGEND mechanics. LEGEND cards are split across two cards that form one artwork. Highly collectible era.

### HS Unleashed (UL)

**Released:** 2010-05-12 · **95 cards** · *HeartGold & SoulSilver Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `iconic`

**Notable cards:** Tyranitar Prime, Steelix Prime, Ursaring Prime, Crobat Prime, Raikou & Suicune LEGEND

Tyranitar Prime is the defining chase of this set. Beloved by Johto collectors.

### HS Undaunted (UD)

**Released:** 2010-08-18 · **90 cards** · *HeartGold & SoulSilver Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `grail` · `eeveelution`

**Notable cards:** Espeon Prime, Umbreon Prime, Tyrogue, Houndoom Prime, Slowking Prime, Entei & Raikou LEGEND

Contains Espeon Prime and Umbreon Prime — two of the most valuable and sought-after modern-vintage cards. Tyrogue also an iconic chase.

### HS Triumphant (TM)

**Released:** 2010-11-03 · **102 cards** · *HeartGold & SoulSilver Series*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage`

**Notable cards:** Mew Prime, Gengar Prime, Celebi Prime, Machamp Prime, Ho-Oh LEGEND (alt)

Mew Prime and Gengar Prime are the headliners. Final HGSS-branded main set.

### Call of Legends (CL)

**Released:** 2011-02-09 · **95 cards** · *Call of Legends*

**Tags:** `modern-vintage` · `transition` · `shiny`

**Mechanics:** Shiny Legendary collection

**Notable cards:** Shiny Suicune, Shiny Raikou, Shiny Entei, Shiny Lugia, Shiny Ho-Oh, Shiny Rayquaza, Shiny Mew

Transition set bridging HGSS and Black & White eras. Features reprint Shiny Legendary Pokemon as secret rares. Treated as its own mini-series.

## Era 5: Black & White Era

**Period:** 2011-2013  
**Publisher:** TPCi  
**Set count:** 12

*Gen 5 / Unova era. Introduced full-art Pokemon and Supporters — the first era where full arts became standard chase targets. Pokemon-EX mechanic reintroduced (replacing LV.X) as the competitive and collector centerpiece. Team Plasma storyline carried through multiple sets. Pokemon TCG Online launched during this era.*

### Black & White (BLW)

**Released:** 2011-04-25 · **114 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `foundational` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** Gen 5 Pokemon, full art cards (first), Poke-Powers/Bodies unified to Abilities

**Notable cards:** Reshiram, Zekrom, Serperior, Emboar, Samurott, Pikachu (full art)

First Gen 5 set. Introduced full-art cards — a visual hallmark still used today. Reshiram and Zekrom debut.

### Emerging Powers (EPO)

**Released:** 2011-08-31 · **98 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern`

**Notable cards:** Samurott, Zoroark, Musharna, Gothitelle

Second B&W set. No Pokemon-EX or special rarities yet. Competitive-focused.

### Noble Victories (NVI)

**Released:** 2011-11-16 · **101 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `iconic-trainer`

**Notable cards:** N (Supporter), Virizion, Terrakion, Cobalion, Victini, Kyurem

N Supporter card from this set became the most iconic Trainer card of the era. Swords of Justice trio featured.

### Next Destinies (NXD)

**Released:** 2012-02-08 · **99 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `mechanical-shift` · `iconic`

**Mechanics:** Pokemon-EX reintroduced (modern)

**Notable cards:** Mewtwo EX, Reshiram EX, Zekrom EX, Regigigas EX, Shaymin EX

Reintroduced Pokemon-EX mechanic in the modern format. Mewtwo EX was the defining chase and competitive staple of the era.

### Dark Explorers (DEX)

**Released:** 2012-05-09 · **108 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `competitive-iconic`

**Notable cards:** Darkrai EX, Tornadus EX, Landorus EX, Hydreigon

Darkrai EX defined the post-2012 meta. Strong competitive set. Landorus EX equally impactful.

### Dragons Exalted (DRX)

**Released:** 2012-08-15 · **124 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `iconic` · `competitive`

**Mechanics:** Dragon type introduced

**Notable cards:** Rayquaza EX, Hydreigon, Garchomp, Altaria

Introduced the Dragon type to the TCG. Rayquaza EX defined a major competitive archetype.

### Dragon Vault (DRV)

**Released:** 2012-10-05 · **20 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `mini-set`

**Notable cards:** Rayquaza, Giratina, Latias, Latios, Dialga

Mini-set with 20 Dragon-type reprints. Sold in small promotional packs, not as a standalone main release.

### Boundaries Crossed (BCR)

**Released:** 2012-11-07 · **149 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `large-set` · `competitive`

**Mechanics:** ACE SPEC Trainers introduced

**Notable cards:** Keldeo EX, Kyurem EX, Black Kyurem, White Kyurem, Blastoise, Landorus

Introduced ACE SPEC — powerful Trainer cards limited to one per deck. Keldeo EX was a competitive keystone paired with Black Kyurem.

### Plasma Storm (PLS)

**Released:** 2013-02-06 · **135 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `team-plasma-arc`

**Mechanics:** Team Plasma subtype

**Notable cards:** Genesect EX (Team Plasma), Thundurus EX, Hydreigon, Team Plasma Basic Energy

Start of Team Plasma three-set arc. Team Plasma Pokemon have unique interactions with themed Trainers.

### Plasma Freeze (PLF)

**Released:** 2013-05-08 · **116 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `team-plasma-arc`

**Notable cards:** Kyurem EX (Team Plasma), Deoxys EX (Team Plasma), Thundurus EX, Absol

Second Team Plasma set. Deoxys EX + Kyurem EX formed competitive archetype.

### Plasma Blast (PLB)

**Released:** 2013-08-14 · **101 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `team-plasma-arc` · `closing-chapter`

**Notable cards:** Ho-Oh EX, Lugia EX (Team Plasma), Dialga EX, Palkia EX

Final Team Plasma set. Ho-Oh EX and Lugia EX closed the era with a Johto legendary callback.

### Legendary Treasures (LTR)

**Released:** 2013-11-06 · **113 cards** · *Black & White Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `compilation` · `shiny`

**Mechanics:** Radiant Collection sub-set

**Notable cards:** Rayquaza EX, Charizard (reverse holo), Meloetta, Radiant Collection sub-set (25 cards)

Compilation set closing the B&W era. Contains the Radiant Collection — 25 bonus cards with shiny/alternate treatments. Precursor concept to later 'trainer gallery' sub-sets.

## Era 6: XY Era

**Period:** 2014-2016  
**Publisher:** The Pokémon Company International  
**Set count:** 15

*Gen VI. Introduced Pokémon-EX with Mega Evolution mechanic (M-EX) and the Fairy type to TCG. 20th anniversary celebrated mid-era with Generations and Evolutions sets. Strong Charizard-chase era (Flashfire, Evolutions). BREAK cards introduced late-era.*

### Kalos Starter Set (KSS)

**Released:** 2013-11-08 · **39 cards** · *XY Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `special` · `starter`

**Mechanics:** introduction of XY-era design

**Notable cards:** Chespin, Fennekin, Froakie, Xerneas, Yveltal

Small preview set released ahead of XY base set. Contained 3 fixed starter decks. Not a standard expansion — primarily teaching/onboarding product.

### XY (XY)

## Era 7: Sun & Moon Era

**Period:** 2017-2019  
**Publisher:** The Pokémon Company International  
**Set count:** 16

*Gen VII. Introduced Pokémon-GX (similar to EX but with GX Attacks — once per game high-impact moves). Special 'Fates' sets revived Shiny Pokémon collecting. Cosmic Eclipse closed the era with TAG TEAM mechanic and largest set of the era.*

### Sun & Moon (SUM)

**Released:** 2017-02-03 · **149 cards** · *Sun & Moon Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `base-set` · `mechanic-debut`

**Notable cards:** Solgaleo-GX, Lunala-GX, Decidueye-GX, Incineroar-GX, Primarina-GX

Base set introducing the GX mechanic. Alolan starter trio as GX cards. Marked a shift in card design and art direction.

### Guardians Rising (GRI)

**Released:** 2017-05-05 · **169 cards** · *Sun & Moon Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `competitive-staple`

**Mechanics:** Island Guardian tapu focus

**Notable cards:** Tapu Lele-GX, Tapu Koko-GX, Lycanroc-GX, Metagross-GX

Tapu Lele-GX became one of the most-played competitive cards of the GX era (its ability to grab Supporter cards). Tapus themed set tied to Alola region.

### Burning Shadows (BUS)

**Mechanics:** Rainbow Rare prominence

**Notable cards:** Charizard-GX (Rainbow Rare), Gardevoir-GX, Golisopod-GX

Rainbow Rare Charizard-GX is one of the signature cards of the Sun & Moon era. Heavy chase value. Gardevoir-GX spawned a defining competitive archetype.

Small special set focusing on Shining variants of Legendary and Mythical Pokémon. Not sold in standard booster boxes — available through special collections and 3-pack blisters.

**Notable cards:** Buzzwole-GX, Guzzlord-GX, Silvally-GX, Necrozma-GX

Ultra Beast themed set from the Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon games. Silvally-GX as legendary ally. Smaller set size than surrounding releases.

### Ultra Prism (UPR)

Introduced Prism Star ♦ cards — unique powerful cards limited to one copy per deck. Full-art Gen IV Trainers (Cynthia, Cyrus) became staples.

### Forbidden Light (FLI)

**Tags:** `modern` · `competitive-staple`

**Mechanics:** Alolan variant focus

Malamar + Ultra Necrozma-GX combo became a dominant Psychic-type archetype. Zygarde-GX also featured.

### Celestial Storm (CES)

**Mechanics:** Full-art Trainer reprints

**Notable cards:** Rayquaza-GX, Zoroark-GX (Shining), Celebi Prism Star, Gible Full Art

Rayquaza-GX became a top-tier competitive deck. Stunning full-art Trainers (Lana, Mallow, Steven) were major collector targets.

Dragon-type themed special set. Every card in the set has a foil treatment. Only sold through special collections and pin blisters — no booster boxes. 70 main cards + 8 secret rares.

### Unified Minds (UNM)

68-card main set + 94-card Shiny Vault sub-set = 162 cards total (plus a secret rare). Shiny Charizard-GX (SV49/SV94) is one of the most iconic modern chase cards. Sealed products (especially tins) have appreciated significantly. Defining 'Fates' line set.

### Cosmic Eclipse (CEC)

**Tags:** `modern` · `largest-set` · `era-closer`

Largest Pokémon TCG set at release (272 cards). Era-closing capstone with three-Pokémon TAG TEAMs and TAG TEAM Supporter cards (paired Trainers on single card). Arceus & Dialga & Palkia-GX saw heavy competitive play.

## Era 8: Sword & Shield Era

**Period:** 2020-2023  
**Publisher:** The Pokémon Company International  
**Set count:** 17

*Gen VIII. Introduced Pokémon V and VMAX (Dynamax/Gigantamax), then VSTAR. The era that coincided with COVID pandemic hobby boom. Evolving Skies produced Moonbreon — arguably the most iconic modern chase card. Alternate Art treatments became a defining collector format. Radiant Pokémon introduced late-era.*

### Sword & Shield (SSH)

**Released:** 2020-02-07 · **216 cards** · *Sword & Shield Series*

**Notable cards:** Zacian V, Zamazenta V, Snorlax V

Base set of Sword & Shield era. Introduced V and VMAX mechanics. Galar region focus. Zacian V and Zamazenta V as legendary starters.

### Rebel Clash (RCL)

**Released:** 2020-05-01 · **209 cards** · *Sword & Shield Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `competitive-staple`

**Mechanics:** VMAX continued

**Notable cards:** Eldegoss V, Boss's Orders (Giovanni), Inteleon VMAX, Rillaboom VMAX

### Darkness Ablaze (DAA)

### Vivid Voltage (VIV)

Pikachu VMAX Rainbow Rare became a top chase card. Introduced Amazing Rare cards (unique etched foil style). Launched during peak pandemic hobby boom.

### Chilling Reign (CRE)

Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX and Ice Rider Calyrex VMAX dominated competitive play for months. First set with widespread alternate art full-body treatments. Crown Tundra DLC tie-in.

### Evolving Skies (EVS)

## Era 9: Scarlet & Violet Era

**Period:** 2023-2025  
**Publisher:** The Pokémon Company International  
**Set count:** 16

*Gen IX. Paldea region. Returned to yellow borders and classic card back design. Pokémon ex (lowercase) replaced V/VSTAR. Illustration Rare and Special Illustration Rare became defining collector formats. Prismatic Evolutions and 151 created hobby-wide chase frenzies. Black Bolt / White Flare split-release format debuted.*

### Scarlet & Violet (SVI)

**Released:** 2023-03-31 · **198 cards** · *Scarlet & Violet Series*

**Tags:** `modern` · `base-set` · `mechanic-debut`

**Mechanics:** Pokémon ex (lowercase) debut, Tera Pokémon mechanic, Illustration Rare debut, return to yellow card borders

**Notable cards:** Koraidon ex, Miraidon ex, Sprigatito, Fuecoco, Quaxly

Base set of Scarlet & Violet era. Reset the card design back to classic yellow borders. Introduced the new Pokémon ex mechanic and Illustration Rare rarity tier. Paldea region starters featured.

### Paldea Evolved (PAL)

**Released:** 2023-06-09 · **279 cards** · *Scarlet & Violet Series*

### Obsidian Flames (OBF)

**Released:** 2023-08-11 · **230 cards** · *Scarlet & Violet Series*

Nostalgia-driven special expansion covering only the original 151 Kanto Pokémon. Launched to massive demand and sustained scarcity. SIR Mew ex is the signature chase. Set sold out at retail for extended periods post-launch. Became a benchmark for how nostalgia + scarcity drives modern sealed demand.

### Paradox Rift (PAR)

Introduced Ancient and Future sub-types (Paradox Pokémon from Area Zero). Mechanics impacted competitive deck building. Roaring Moon ex saw extended tournament relevance.

### Paldean Fates (PAF)

91 main card numbers but 245 cards total due to Shiny subset. Continuation of the Hidden Fates / Shining Fates lineage. Bubble Mew (Shiny illustration rare) became a signature chase. Shiny variants of existing Scarlet & Violet cards with unique art treatments.

**Mechanics:** Ancient/Future Paradox continued

**Notable cards:** Iron Crown ex (Future Paradox), Raging Bolt ex (Ancient Paradox), Gouging Fire ex

Continuation of Paradox Pokémon theme from Paradox Rift. Raging Bolt ex and Gouging Fire ex became key competitive cards.

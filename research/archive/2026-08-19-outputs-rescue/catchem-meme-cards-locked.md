# What this batch teaches us about voice

**The five Tyler edits taught more than the original drafts:**

1. **LGS owner:** *"i'll give you 45%. but it's trade-in value. i gotta run a business."*
2. **Brad's cancellation:** flipped victim/villain framing
3. **Rip-and-ship guy:** *"suddenly ends stream after chase hit. chat refunded."*
4. **Awkward teenager and mom:** *"trade up challenge. base set energy card."*
5. **The toploader:** *"i held your grail since 1999. it's more mine than yours."*

**Pattern:** Replace observational descriptions with specific quotes/behaviors collectors recognize. Voice from INSIDE the character, not ABOUT the character.

**Voice principles confirmed:**
- Names carry half the weight ("zard tax" says it before you read flavor)
- Flavor is ONE BEAT, no setup-payoff structure
- Real collector specifics (45% trade-in, ends stream, base set energy) beat clever wordplay
- Slight unhinged energy, never bro-loud
- All lowercase

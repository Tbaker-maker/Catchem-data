Real talk before you queue up, because we'd rather under-promise:

⚙️ **This is the raw-bones build — on purpose.** The full game is in and functioning end-to-end, but what you're loading into is the skeleton: placeholder visuals, minimal animation, rough edges everywhere. If it looks unfinished, that's because it is.

🔧 **Expect constant change.** Balance, numbers, mechanics, maybe whole systems — nothing is sacred yet. What you play this week may not be what you play next week. That's the point of this phase.

🎯 **Our philosophy: gameplay first, paint later.** We're not touching serious visuals until the game plays *smooth* and *competitive*. A pretty game that feels bad is dead on arrival — so the polish comes once the bones prove themselves.

🗣️ **What actually helps us right now:** play it, try to break it, and tell us how it *feels* — what's clunky, what's unfair, what made you say "one more round." Feel/balance feedback > "add graphics" (we know 😄).

Thanks for stepping into the ugly phase with us — the people here now are the ones shaping what this becomes. 🫡

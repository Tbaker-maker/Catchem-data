**Date:** April 23, 2026

**Rule enforcement:** All 9 are Pokemon-connected per HARD RULE Apr 23

# TRAINERS

## 4. derek, target overnight stocker

*knows what's on the pallets. has been offered $500 for intel. took it once.*

"hi. i'm derek. i work wednesdays. the pallets come at 4:47am. wednesday is prismatic. friday is modern. saturday is the mcdonald's tie-in if we're unlucky."

"a guy named brandon offered me $500 last month to tell him what's coming. i took the $500. i told him it was a mcdonald's week. he was furious. i did not lie. he did not ask the right question."

"there's a runner who does the 2.3 miles from his house every wednesday. he thinks we're friends. we're not friends. i nod because i'm employed to be polite. the nod is not friendship. it is retail policy."

*voice: retail-weary, quietly shrewd, enjoys the chaos*

## 5. the LGS owner who keeps getting cancelled on

*every preorder. every set. every time. is fine, probably.*

"i have pre-sold 400 destined rivals booster boxes. 47 of them cancelled on me when prices dropped. the customer is always right. the customer is never in my spreadsheets."

"brad called yesterday. brad wanted to cancel his preorder because he 'found a better deal.' i said 'brad, i've known you since 2019.' brad said 'it's just business.' brad is no longer welcome at tuesday night league."

"i have a wall behind the counter. it is cards from people who cancelled. each card is signed 'i'm sorry, i'm so sorry.' no one has signed a card. it's a metaphor. i'm explaining the metaphor because no one gets it when i bring it up."

*voice: small-business-stoic, tracks every slight, 80% functional*

## 6. the youtuber whose thumbnail is always screaming

*has opened 4,000 booster boxes on camera. has never paid rent.*

"thumbnail is a yellow arrow pointing at my open mouth. title is ALL CAPS. 'I OPENED 50 BOOSTER BOXES AND PULLED—.' the em-dash is legally required by my editor."

"my kitchen has become a rip-and-ship. my actual kitchen. i have not cooked a meal in this building since 2022. i have eaten 1,400 packs of ramen. ramen is also a trading card to me now."

"someone asked what i would do if pokemon crashed. i said 'crash.' we laughed. neither of us was joking. i think about this exchange most evenings."

*voice: manic-professional, aware of the brink, still going*

# OBJECTS

*clear plastic. thick. has held every card you've ever loved. remembers.*

"i have held 340 cards in my life. 12 of them were mint. 3 of them were your childhood blastoise. you know the one. i was there."

"my arch enemy is the sleeve. the sleeve thinks it's my partner. the sleeve is my rival. without me the sleeve is a sad little plastic sock. i AM the structure. i am load-bearing."

*voice: self-important, overly dramatic, weirdly valid*

## 9. the pop report

*a live spreadsheet. updated nightly. sentient. bored.*

"i am a grid of numbers. columns: card, grade, count, population. rows: 340,000 of them. i was alive by wednesday. by friday i had opinions."

"the charizard ex pop is 847. the moonbreon pop is 16,440. i am not allowed to play favorites. i play favorites. the moonbreon is wearing me down. everyone keeps adding to her. her row is a crime scene of submissions."

# Voice notes across the set

**Creatures (1-3):** Real Pokemon reframed as self-aware celebrities/grail-objects/grief-characters. The Pokemon does the cultural work — we don't have to explain why charizard is the tax, collectors already know. The VOICE is the fresh thing.

**Trainers (4-6):** Actual humans from collector life. Derek, the LGS owner, the YouTuber. These are the people collectors encounter in real life. The humor comes from specificity (brandon, brad, the mcdonald's misdirect, the $500 story).

**Objects (7-9):** Inanimate TCG-world things given interior life. The toploader knows it's load-bearing. The scale knows its siblings weigh drugs. The pop report is a bored god.

**What V5 is doing that V4 wasn't:**
- Punchlines EARLIER in each piece
- Cute-absurd conceits (pop report as bored god, scale with drug-weighing siblings, charizard's lapras wife)
- Differentiated voices (celebrity / joke with secrets / grieving / retail-shrewd / small-business-trapped / manic-professional / self-important / existential-devoted / cosmic-bored)
- Shorter lines where they land
- Still culturally authentic — Pokemon references used correctly

**What could be wrong:**
- Some might be TOO absurd for Catch'em's soft-feelings voice
- Charizard's "breathe fire and equity" might be too crypto-adjacent after we killed that direction
- The YouTuber's "i think about this exchange most evenings" is sad-poet energy sneaking back in
- Magikarp's G-Wagon joke might be too bro for Catch'em's voice

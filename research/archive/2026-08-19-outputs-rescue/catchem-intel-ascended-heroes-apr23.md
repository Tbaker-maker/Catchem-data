## Alex's structural thesis (this is analysis, not fact)

### Why Ascended Heroes is different from Prismatic Evolutions

**Prismatic Evolutions (Jan 2025) was cushioned:**
- Journey Together (hype absorber — partner Pokemon teaser at Worlds, first $200 booster box)
- Destined Rivals (hype absorber — built up for a year)
- Black Bolt / White Flare (hype absorber)
- → Pressure distributed across multiple sets, collectors had options

**Ascended Heroes (Feb 2026) is alone:**
- Phantasmal Flames preceded it — Charizard-focused but not heavily ripped
- Perfect Order (March 2026) — "no one cares about Perfect Order" (low chase value)
- Chaos Rising (May 22) — presale dropped to $245, trending low
- Pitch Black (later) — no clear hype
- 30th Anniversary set — too far out
- → All hobby attention concentrated on one set

### The sentiment shift

Prismatic Evolutions triggered **angry public backlash** — enough that Pokemon Company officially announced reprints.

Ascended Heroes is triggering **resigned acceptance**. Collectors are still talking about high prices, but they're not demanding reprints. The hobby has normalized $150 ETBs. This is a structural shift in expectations that will affect every future set release.

### The open-vs-hold calculus

Alex's claim: because singles and PSA 10s outpace pack cost, Ascended Heroes is one of the few current sets where **ripping makes more EV sense than sealing**. Drama channels disagreed with this call at release, but prices on singles went UP not DOWN post-release, validating him.

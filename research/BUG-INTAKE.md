## THE WORKFLOW CHANGE
One bug per turn is the most expensive ratio possible, and it hides
patterns. Instead: Tyler adds a line here whenever he sees something
wrong, and chat clears the whole list in one pass.

WHY IT IS BETTER, not just cheaper:
- Bugs seen TOGETHER reveal their class. The `x1` filter bug and the
  four-box photo looked unrelated one at a time. Together they are both
  "a filter written by someone who could not see what it excluded."
- A batch gets one round of guards, one audit, one push — instead of
  five of each.
- Nothing gets lost between sessions. A note here survives; a note in
  chat does not.

## HOW TO ADD ONE (10 seconds, phone is fine)
Just a line. Where, and what looked wrong. No formatting required.
    - board: evolutions etb photo shows 4 boxes
    - daily three: cards different heights
    - destined rivals packs: only 13 listings, way too low

## THE STANDING RULE
Every cleared bug asks one question before it closes: **what CLASS is
this, and what guard stops the class?** Fixing the instance is not the
lesson. That question is why we have twenty safeguards instead of twenty
patched symptoms.

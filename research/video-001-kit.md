talking — and I'm raffling this to prove it." / "Every message you send
in my server has a chance to win Pokémon. Here's how." / "No sub-gate.
No paywall. Just talk, earn berries, win cardboard."
BEATS (60-120s short or 4-6min long):
1. COLD OPEN: hold the prize (House Vault item). One line: what it is +
   "verified at $X by my own price engine — receipts on screen."
2. THE MECHANIC (screen-rec the Lab): type one real message → Daily
   Berry drops 🍓 on camera. "First real message every day = guaranteed
   berry. Keep talking, small chance of bonus drops. That's it."
3. THE FREEZER: show Fresh vs Frozen in one breath — "spend the fresh
   ones on raffles; the frozen ones never leave — they're literally
   your voting power on what I raffle next. You can't buy either."
4. THE RAFFLE: show the live raffle embed — verified value chip, entry
   button, the provably-fair line. "When it draws, the seed and winner
   index are public — check my math."
5. CTA: "Entry's free — it's behind one wall: join and say something.
   Link below." (NO sub-begging — the wall IS the Discord.)

catchemtcg.com — methodology public" · not-sponsored line.

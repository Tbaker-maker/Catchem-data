PSA's Value services remain paused.
 Sources: [CardGrade (PSA)](https://cardgrade.io/blog/psa-value-tiers-paused-2026) + [CardGrade (backlog)](https://cardgrade.io/blog/card-grading-backlog-2026). **Matters for Catch'em:** With 30th Celebration dropping Sept 16 and an all-foil set expected to flood grading queues, the PSA Value pause means collectors must budget $79.99+ minimum per card — a meaningful friction point for sealed-to-graded pipeline analysis.

**GRADING_NEWS (TAG — limited drop expected late August)** — 
TAG's July 29 public update reports a backlog load of ~189% of monthly processing capacity, which TAG says is 55% below its peak of ~420%.
 
TAG anticipates a limited submission drop near 80% backlog recovery, with a current estimate of **late August**, and full catch-up by end of September.
 Source: [CardGrade backlog guide](https://cardgrade.io/blog/card-grading-backlog-2026). **Matters for Catch'em:** A TAG re-open window landing the same week as Ascended Heroes / Mega Forces Tins (Aug 28) could drive grading interest in AH pulls.

## Notable but Not Urgent

- **30th Celebration grading queue surge expected** — 
The all-foil 30th Celebration set is expected to flood grading queues the way 2021's Celebrations did, but at a larger scale.
 [PreGradeCards](https://pregradecards.com/blog/pokemon-delta-reign-30th-celebration-grading-guide-fall-2026). Catch'em angle: proactive content on PSA/CGC tier selection before Sept 16.

- **CGC bulk turnaround currently 150 working days** — 
CGC lists 150 working days for Bulk and 90 for Economy as of August 4, 2026.
 [CardGrade/CGC guide](https://cardgrade.io/blog/cgc-grading-cost-turnaround-guide). A Bulk submission today on a 30th Celebration pull would not return until mid-2027.

- **Collectors Holdings / PSA-SGC-Beckett consolidation watch** — 
As of April 2026, Collectors Holdings owns PSA and SGC and has a pending agreement to acquire Beckett (BGS), which would give a single company control of roughly 80% of the trading card grading market by volume.
 [OrbSportsCards](https://orbsportscards.com/psa-vs-bgs-vs-sgc-vs-cgc-grading-comparison-2026/). No new developments today — ongoing watch item.

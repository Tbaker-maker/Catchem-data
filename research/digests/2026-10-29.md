# Research Digest: 2026-10-29

## 🚨 Flags
- GRADING_NEWS: PSA reopened its Value tier today, Oct 29 at 8 AM PT, in the US, Canada, Europe and Japan. It costs $39.99 US / $56.99 CAD per card with a $500 max insured value and an estimated 90 to 100 business days (120 in Japan), and new Value orders queue behind everything already at PSA. For new orders since Oct 28, Standard ($59.99) drops to 70 to 80 business days and Priority ($79.99) to 50 to 60. The backlog is 7.2M units as of the Oct 20 update, down from 8.2M on Oct 6. Value Bulk stays paused until the backlog reaches about 5M. Why it matters for Catch'em: cheaper grading returns for the first time since May, which can lift submission volume and raw-to-graded spreads on mid-value cards, and the grading notes in prior digests (Value paused) are now stale. https://www.psacard.com/articles/articleview/15779/psa-reopens-value-service-level · https://www.psacard.com/info/submission-updates · https://www.psacard.com/info/backlog-tracker · https://sportscollectorsdigest.com/psa-reopens-value-grading-service-reduces-turnaround-times-amid-massive-backlog

- **Delta Reign fully revealed (Aug 20) by TPCi press release and PokeBeach.**

Prereleases will run October 24th through November 1st.
 Sources: [pokemon.com](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/the-pokemon-tcg-mega-evolution-delta-reign-expansion-arrives-november-6-2026), [PokeBeach](https://www.pokebeach.com/2026/08/delta-reign-tcg-set-officially-revealed-for-november-full-product-lineup)

The set's Elite Trainer Box will feature the Kyogre Illustration Rare originally from Storm Emeralda,
 cut from the main set and repurposed as an exclusive ETB pull. 
Legendary Stadium cards are introduced for this set — stadium cards that require combining two Stadium cards, played as a pair, creating an extended artwork when placed next to each other.
 Sources: [PokeBeach](https://www.pokebeach.com/2026/08/delta-reign-tcg-set-officially-revealed-for-november-full-product-lineup), [PokeGuardian](https://www.pokeguardian.com/3231721_pokemon-tcg-mega-evolution-delta-reign-revealed)

The EN set should include 110+ cards from Japan's Storm Emeralda set plus 30 unique cards from the "MEGA Starter Decks," though one of the Pokémon ex from the decks will be cut from Delta Reign.
 
This will be the first English set since Phantasmal Flames to be a combination of multiple Japanese sources.
 Source: [PokeBeach](https://www.pokebeach.com/2026/08/delta-reign-tcg-set-officially-revealed-for-november-full-product-lineup)

The latest visible update, dated July 28, 2026, reports 12.4 million units, up from 11 million on July 14.
 
Projections for the Value tier's return have slipped from an October estimate toward winter.

The Destined Rivals reprint could be one of the most meaningful Pokemon supply events of 2026, with booster boxes and ETBs expected to begin arriving near the end of August or early September.
 
Prices are already responding: booster boxes have fallen significantly from their highs, while regular ETBs have lost approximately half their peak value.

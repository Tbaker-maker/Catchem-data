## 🚨 Flags

**GRADING_NEWS — Beckett Base/Standard grading temporarily paused; expected reopen Sep 15, 2026**

Beckett Collectibles placed a temporary pause on accepting online grading submissions for their Base and Standard Card Grading services as of early August, with those tiers expected to reopen September 15, 2026. The pause was enacted to process existing orders, improve turnaround times, and expand grading capacity.
 Source: [ICv2, Aug 6 2026](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63045/beckett-pumps-brakes-accepting-online-grading-submissions) — **Matters for Catch'em:** Beckett graded TCG cards (including Mega Evolution era Pokémon) can't be submitted at base tiers until mid-September; affects supply of fresh slabs heading into the 30th Celebration wave. Human verify reopen date.

**GRADING_NEWS — PSA Value tier backlog surged; reopening now projected for winter, not October**

The PSA Value tier has been halted since June 2. PSA's baseline entry is now Regular service at $80/card with a 40–50 business day turnaround. The backlog has been declining but projections for the Value tier's return have slipped from an October estimate toward winter.
 Source: [TheCardShopFinder, ~Aug 2026](https://thecardshopfinder.com/blog/card-grading-august-2026/) — **Matters for Catch'em:** Value tier delayed further than previously forecasted; bulk submitters remain shut out through 30th Celebration launch.

**GRADING_NEWS — PSA backlog at 12.4M units as of July 28 (up from 11M on July 14)**

The latest PSA Backlog Tracker update, dated July 28, 2026, reports 12.4 million units in queue — up from 11 million on July 14. Reopening is tied to reducing the active backlog to 5 million units.
 Source: [CardGrade.io](https://cardgrade.io/blog/psa-value-tiers-paused-2026) — **Matters for Catch'em:** Backlog is moving in the wrong direction; reopening math is very unfavorable heading into Q4 surge.

**GRADING_NEWS — Collectors Holdings / Beckett acquisition: pending deal faces antitrust pressure**

Collectors Holdings (owner of PSA and SGC) has a pending deal to acquire Beckett/BGS, announced December 2025, which would put three of the four major graders — roughly 80% of grading volume — under one corporate parent. CGC remains independent. The Beckett deal faces an active antitrust lawsuit in California and a request for an FTC investigation, and has not formally closed.
 Source: [OrbSportsCards](https://orbsportscards.com/psa-vs-bgs-vs-sgc-vs-cgc-grading-comparison-2026/) — **Matters for Catch'em:** If/when it closes, pricing consolidation across PSA/SGC/Beckett is the biggest structural grading story of 2026; reshapes competitive landscape for CGC.

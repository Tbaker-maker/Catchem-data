## New since yesterday

- **Delta Reign officially fully revealed (Aug 20):** 
The expansion includes over 135 cards, with more than 20 Trainer cards and over 35 cards with special illustrations. It features Mega Rayquaza ex, Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex, and Mega Golisopod ex.
 
Prereleases will take place October 24–November 1.
 
The ETB promo will be the Kyogre Illustration Rare originally from Storm Emeralda, cut from Delta Reign's main card pool. The set also introduces new split Stadium cards, each with "Legendary" in their names.
 Source: [pokemon.com](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/the-pokemon-tcg-mega-evolution-delta-reign-expansion-arrives-november-6-2026), [PokeBeach](https://www.pokebeach.com/2026/08/delta-reign-tcg-set-officially-revealed-for-november-full-product-lineup)

- **Delta Reign is a multi-JP-source set:** 
This will be the first English set since Phantasmal Flames to be a combination of multiple Japanese sources — all other recent sets have been nearly 1:1 copies of their equivalent Japanese set.
 Source: [PokeBeach](https://www.pokebeach.com/2026/08/delta-reign-tcg-set-officially-revealed-for-november-full-product-lineup)

- **Delta Reign set code still unofficial:** 
The set code is still unknown; sellers are using ME06, but Pokémon has not officially confirmed it.
 Source: [HouseOfCarts](https://dashboard.houseofcarts.io/resources/pokemon-delta-reign-release-guide)

- **Beckett Base/Standard submissions paused:** 
Beckett cited strong year-over-year growth from July 2025 to July 2026 as the driver: cards submitted rose 102%, TCG cards graded increased 159%, and new collectors grew 65%.
 
As checked August 8, 2026, Beckett says new online Base and Standard submissions are paused. It expects to begin reopening those services on September 15, 2026. Existing and in-transit orders continue processing. Express and Priority remain available.
 Source: [Sports Collectors Daily](https://www.sportscollectorsdaily.com/beckett-joins-psa-in-pause-of-lower-cost-grading-services/), [CardGrade.io](https://cardgrade.io/psa-vs-beckett)

- **PSA backlog worsening — Value reopening pushed to winter:** 
PSA's Backlog Tracker says reopening is tied to reducing the active backlog to 5 million units. The latest visible update, dated July 28, 2026, reports 12.4 million units, up from 11 million on July 14 — making this a status page, not a countdown.
 
Estimates have moved from October toward winter.
 Source: [CardGrade.io](https://cardgrade.io/blog/psa-value-tiers-paused-2026), [TheCardShopFinder](https://thecardshopfinder.com/blog/card-grading-august-2026/)

- **CGC under surge pressure from grading market consolidation:** 
Per GemRate, the industry graded a record 3.5 million cards in June 2026, with daily volume staying elevated through July — a recent single-day figure came in at 107,073 cards graded across all major graders.
 
Heavy submission volume has pushed CGC Economy turnaround to 60–100 business days, with Standard service at 35–55 days.
 Source: [TheCardShopFinder](https://thecardshopfinder.com/blog/card-grading-august-2026/)

## Notable but not urgent

- **Grading market consolidation + antitrust watch:** 
Collectors Holdings acquired PSA in 2021, bought SGC in 2024, and announced a deal for Beckett in December 2025 — roughly 80% of the trading card grading market by volume under a single corporate parent.
 
Representative Pat Ryan of New York sent a letter to the FTC in early 2026 asking for an investigation into whether Collectors has engaged in monopolization of the grading market, and a separate private antitrust lawsuit has also been filed.
 Outcome TBD — worth monitoring for any rulings or FTC action.

- **Delta Reign preorders expected ~Oct (one month out):** 
Prereleases run October 24–November 1; preorders should open on PokemonCenter.com in September, about a month before release, based on timing of the last two sets.
 Flag for Catch'em preorder tracker.

- **30th Celebration 160+ card count confirmed:** 
The set features 160+ cards — every single card holofoil — with 30 unique Pikachu variants and a Classic Collection subset reprinting Base Set icons including Charizard.
 This is higher than the 150+ count previously circulating; worth verifying against official TPCi checklist when released.

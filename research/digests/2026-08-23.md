- **Delta Reign — "Legendary Stadiums" mechanic confirmed:** 
Delta Reign introduces "Legendary Stadiums," which are Stadium cards played together as a pair with combined illustrations. Any booster pack containing a Legendary Stadium will contain both halves of the pair.
 Three Legendary Stadiums are in the set — a new sealed-product consideration since dual-Stadium packs change pack-value calculations. Source: [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Delta_Reign_(TCG)).

- **Delta Reign — multiple Japanese sources confirmed:** 
Delta Reign merges the 110+ cards from Storm Emeralda, cards from the MEGA Starter Decks released alongside it in Japan, and previously unreleased Gym promos like Grisham, Mega Tatsugiri ex, and Lida.
 
This will be the first English set since Phantasmal Flames to be a combination of multiple Japanese sources.
 Source: [TotalCards/PokeBeach](https://totalcards.net/blogs/pokemon/delta-reign-tcg-set-officially-revealed-for-november-full-product-lineup).

The set's Elite Trainer Box will feature the Kyogre Illustration Rare originally from Storm Emeralda
, cut from the main set and repurposed as an ETB-exclusive promo (MEP 117, Pokémon Center stamped). Source: [PokeBeach](https://www.pokebeach.com/2026/08/delta-reign-tcg-set-officially-revealed-for-november-full-product-lineup), [Bill's Archive](https://billsarchive.com/delta-reign.html).

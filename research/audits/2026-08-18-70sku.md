## Finding: me1 Enhanced ($332.40) vs regular ($342.37) inversion — GENUINE

Not a filter defect. Both title sets are 10/10 correct and disjoint:

Regular keeps (sample): "ME01 Mega Evolution BOOSTER BOX Factory Sealed 36
Packs REGULAR" $319.95 · "Mega Evolution Base Set Sealed Booster Box ME01
2025" $309 · "ME01 Booster Box - English 36 Packs" $319.95 — range $295–350.

Enhanced keeps (sample): "ME01 Enhanced Booster Box Sealed New" $339.99 ·
"Enhanced Booster Box Sealed ME01 Promo" $309 · "Enhanced Booster Box
Bulbasaur Promo" $299 — range $299–350.

The ranges overlap almost entirely; the $10 median gap is noise. **No
box-topper premium is currently being paid for me1 Enhanced.** Enhanced also
has the cleaner supply signal (29 fetched/27 kept dedicated listings vs
regular's 26 survivors from 89 noisy fetches). Editorial angle candidate.

## Tuning commit `935c282` — what changed and why (all dated evidence)

1. **etb subtype excludes "pokemon center"** — $127.99 "ETB Pokemon Center
   Exclusive" kept in me5-etb inflated its median $85→$100. pc-etb REQUIREs
   the phrase, so the SKU families are now disjoint. (me5-etb → $77.99.)
2. **Multi-lot tokens generalized** — 2x/x2/3x/x3/4x/x4/6x/x6 moved to
   EXCLUDE_COMMON; were booster-box-only and single-spelling. Evidence:
   "$142.99 Bundle x2. 12 Packs" kept in me2-bb; "$229.99 2x Factory Sealed"
   kept in me3-pc-etb.
3. **Damage plurals/bare forms** — dents, rips, worn, damage, distressed.
   Evidence: "Small rips or dents" (me5-etb), "Box Damage" (me4-pc-etb),
   "Box is Worn" (me2pt5-etb), "(Distressed Box)" (me5-bb).
4. **Mystery/accessory exclusions** — mystery, outer sleeve, card sleeves.
   Evidence: sv3pt5-etb's entire kept set was 3 mystery-box gambles + sleeve
   accessories.
5. **me1-pc-etb per-SKU excludes** — three $420–446 two-box "Lucario &
   Gardevoir / ETB Set" listings kept; excluded; median $230 n=14.
6. **sv3pt5-etb reclassified thin → CLASS B** — the [30,400] window sat
   entirely below the real 151 ETB market. Rebounded to [150,1200]:
   median **$600, n=131, 10/10 genuine** (revalidation titles: "$550 151
   Elite Trainer Box ETB Sealed", "$535 Factory Sealed NEW", …). Third
   class-B ceiling caught by validation this cycle (after EvSkies BB and
   Hidden Fates ETB). Ceiling marked LOW-CONFIDENCE upward if the halo
   keeps climbing.

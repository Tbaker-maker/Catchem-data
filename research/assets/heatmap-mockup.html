i How heat is measured

Volume is inferred, not directly measured. Catch'em tracks active listings on eBay with a daily snapshot. Listings that disappear between snapshots are scored as inferred sales — high confidence for Buy It Now at typical prices, lower confidence for auctions and outliers.

Heat states (🔥 / 📈 / ❄️ / 😴) measure week-over-week trends with a ±10% threshold. They tell you whether money and supply are moving — not exact dollar amounts.

Current sets with high listing turnover are most accurate. Vintage sealed (Hidden Fates, Burning Shadows tins, etc.) has sparse data and may show "thin data" labels instead of states.

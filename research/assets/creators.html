We do not write your copy. The post that worked did partly because the words were the creator's own — two conversational lines, no numbers in them. A hedge in a post reads as a lack of conviction, and borrowed copy reads as borrowed. The image carries the claim; the words carry the feeling, and the feeling has to be yours.

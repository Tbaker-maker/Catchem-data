— "market divergence and demand-pressure signals," PSA-10 premium movement
charts, pack-math set leaderboards ("best bang to rip"), generation blended

+ "grading intensity" model w/ Google Trends inputs; watchlists, community

— surface is chart-shaped and EMPTY; singles-first (sealed shallow);

methodology page); no voice; $400/mo hosting, no revenue; solo bus-factor.

baselines DAILY (index-100 anchored today; premium table live; depth reads
calibrating). Every day both machines run, our charts fill while his say
"not enough history." Plus: sealed depth (168 products, loose-pack anchors,
lifecycle, venue gates), receipts, falsifier-public theses vs black-box IQ.
THREAT LEVEL: highest. He ships fast and thinks in the same instruments.
Response = out-history him, out-sealed him, out-voice him. Not a race on
features — a race on ACCUMULATED TIME × trust.

## COLLECTR (consumer king — 2M+ users)
AMAZING: scanner UX (point camera → set/number/variant), portfolio-as-
stock-chart, Trade Analyzer fairness meter (save/revisit trades), social
showcases + creator follows, 5yr history in Pro ($4.99/mo annual), legendary
support culture (added a product for one emailer), 25+ TCGs breadth.
FALLS SHORT (their own reviews): portfolio value ≠ realizable value —
"you won't get market price… eBay has 13% fees… your collection is worth
less than the number posted"; sealed/vintage/JP-promo gaps; CN sets absent;
scan accuracy poor outside Pokemon; social flaky; portfolio machinery
overkill for casuals; Pro "not worth it" per some.
OUR COUNTER: **Net Proceeds Truth** — a toggle they structurally can't
ship first without deflating 2M portfolios: "market $X · if sold on eBay
today ≈ $Y after fees+shipping." Their top complaint = our honesty flex.
Plus sealed-first authority (their weakest inventory class) + venue-honest
vintage. We don't fight the scanner; we own the MARKET layer.
